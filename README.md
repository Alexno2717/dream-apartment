# Our Apartment

A tiny top-down apartment game — Alex and Rose live here.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or play the live version (link in repo Pages).

## Controls
- **Arrow keys / WASD** — walk
- **Tab** — switch between Alex and Rose (the other becomes an NPC)
- **Space / E** — interact with whatever you're next to
- **M** — sing your character's song
- **H** — heart-bomb the other one

## Things to try
- Walk Alex to the bed and press Space → lullaby
- Walk Rose to the bathroom mirror and press Space → villain anthem
- Get both on the sofa or both in bed at once → big heart boost
- Water the balcony plants, raid the fridge, lounge in the bath
