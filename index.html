how to play

This is your apartment. You're playing as Alex (blonde) by default. Rose (dark hair) wanders around doing her own thing — tap Tab to switch who you control. The other one becomes the NPC.

↑ ← ↓ → / WASDwalk
Space / Einteract with whatever you're standing next to
Tabswitch between Alex & Rose
Msing your song (Alex's lullaby / Rose's villain anthem)
Qsay one of your classic lines
Kkiss the other one (must be close)
Hheart-bomb the other one (cheesy)
Bbuild mode — buy & place furniture, move things around
Xdelete the selected piece (in build mode)
Escclose this

Hearts go up when you interact with stuff and especially when you're together — both on the sofa, both in bed, etc. Walk Alex over to bed and press space → lullaby. Walk Rose to the mirror → villain song.
