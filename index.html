how to play

tab between alex (blonde) and rose (dark hair). the one you're not playing wanders around as an npc.

↑ ← ↓ → / WASDwalk
Space / Einteract with whatever you're standing next to
Tabswitch between alex & rose
Msing your character's song
Qsay a random classic line
Kkiss the other one (when you're close)
Btoggle build mode (move furniture)
Rrotate furniture in build mode
Escclose menus

on a phone: drag on the left half to walk. tap A to interact. ♥ to kiss. tap furniture in build mode.
